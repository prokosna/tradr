## Next three actions

1. **Nothing advertises any more, and that is half of M7's completion criterion.** The probe was the only thing that ever called `BleAdvertiser`, DCR-107 deleted it, and `AndroidBleAdvertiser` is now constructed nowhere -- so Android is undiscoverable over BLE and `BluerAdvertiser` cannot take its place, because R1's controller refuses every advertisement. **The row is the rotation loop and it needs a DCR before it needs an Implementer**: docs/03 says a device holding several secrets "advertises them in turn" and gives that phrase no interval, and docs/11's step 5 stops the bootstrap EID once an ABK exists while [docs/03's asymmetry](docs/03-discovery-and-transport.md#what-that-set-is-made-of-and-the-one-asymmetry-with-advertising) keeps it in the *matching* set -- so what is unspecified is the schedule, not the payload. **`start` replaces rather than fails, which is the contract that makes a rotation expressible**, and the 15-minute window is the one thing that is settled. **`WI-M7-009` is what makes this the first action rather than the second**: Linux can now dial a `ble-gatt` candidate and Android already accepts one, so advertising is the only piece of "Linux and Android exchange text with all Wi-Fi off" that no code performs.

2. **Phase 2's prefilter is written, nothing calls it, and `WI-M7-009` is what turned that from a gap into a defect.** `prefilter` and `BLE_GATT_MAX_TRANSFER_BYTES` have sat in `crates/tradr-transport/src/selection.rs` since M1 with no caller, which cost nothing while no `ble-gatt` candidate could be dialled -- a rule that drops a candidate nothing would have dialled anyway. **Now a peer reachable only over BLE is dialled whatever the transfer weighs**, and docs/03's own Phase 2 says a transfer over 512 KiB drops that candidate. **What the row has to find is the byte count**, and it is not at the call site: `send_files` holds a `Vec<String>` of paths and the sizes are the `Vfs`'s, read one item at a time while building the Offer -- so either the prefilter runs after a size pass the dial currently precedes, or the dial moves after it. That ordering is the design question and it belongs in a DCR before an Implementer sees it.

3. **`WI-M7-005c`'s remaining half still needs a transmitter, and the instrument for it has changed hands.** What can transmit this design's advertisement is an Android phone; a Mac cannot (DCR-103, [ADR-0021](docs/adr/0021-macos-is-scan-only-on-ble.md)); the one phone here cannot hear itself. **So the remaining instruments are a second Android device or a USB BLE controller that does not refuse advertisements the way R1's does** -- a purchase rather than a Work Item. **What is new is what the run will be read from.** The probe printed a line per report and is gone, so the instrument is now the pump's own `ble discovery: arrival handle=...` lines, which appear only for an advertisement whose EID matched a secret this device holds -- a stronger signal than the probe's and a narrower one, since a Tradr peer that matches nothing is now silent by design rather than logged. `adb` cannot reach a phone from this machine, so every run instruction goes to a person at a different machine and has to carry the package name, the `-g` on `adb install`, and what raw output to bring back.
